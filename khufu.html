The ancient companion

Meet Khufu

Named for the pharaoh who aligned his great pyramid to the stars, Khufu is a hairless Sphynx and a registered therapy cat. He's curious, social, and playful, and his warmth helps the therapy room feel a little less intimidating.

He only joins sessions for clients who want him there. If you'd like a cat-free hour, just tell Anne. Khufu is happy to nap somewhere else.

Allergies? Sphynx cats are not hypoallergenic. Let Anne know and Khufu will sit your sessions out.
