ADHD therapy in Grand Blanc, Michigan

ADHD is a different way of working, and a world built on clocks and deadlines can make it feel like a personal failing. Therapy with Anne builds strategies that fit your brain and loosens the shame that years of being called lazy or careless can leave behind.

Call (810) 246-1948 Email Anne

What people often bring to sessions

  • Starting tasks, finishing tasks, and losing track of time
  • Feeling overwhelmed by everything that has to happen at once
  • Strong emotions and rejection sensitivity
  • Shame, self-criticism, and the feeling of falling short
  • Strain in relationships, school, or work
  • Making sense of a diagnosis that came later in life

How Anne works

Sessions focus on practical tools you will actually use, shaped around your interests, energy, and real schedule. That can include setting up outside structure such as reminders and visual plans, breaking big tasks into first steps, and planning for the hard parts of your day.

Just as important is the emotional side. Anne helps you separate who you are from the struggles ADHD can create, so you can treat yourself with more patience while you build new habits.

ADHD rarely travels alone

ADHD often shows up alongside anxiety, depression, or autism. Anne works with the whole picture, so you do not have to sort your experience into separate boxes before you walk in.

Common questions

Does Anne prescribe ADHD medication?

No. Anne is a therapist and does not prescribe. Therapy works well alongside medication if you take it, and with your written permission Anne can coordinate with your prescriber.

Do I need a diagnosis to start?

No. Many people start therapy because they suspect ADHD. A formal diagnosis is not required to work on what is getting in your way.
