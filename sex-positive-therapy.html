Sex-positive care for adults

Sex-positive and kink-friendly therapy in Grand Blanc, Michigan

Talk about sex, desire, kink, and relationships without shame and without having to explain yourself. Anne does not treat consensual adult choices as problems to fix.

Call (810) 246-1948 Email Anne

Who this is for

Adults who are kinky, polyamorous, ethically non-monogamous, asexual, questioning, or simply want a therapist they can be fully honest with about sex and relationships. The focus is on consent, communication, and what you want for your own life.

What people often bring to sessions

  • Opening a relationship, or adjusting the structure you have
  • Jealousy, compersion, and managing time and energy across partners
  • Deciding whether and how to tell others about kink or non-monogamy
  • Shame from religious, cultural, or family messages about sex
  • Differences in desire with a partner
  • Boundaries, consent, and negotiation

How Anne works

Anne listens without judgment and does not assume your kink or relationship style caused whatever brought you in. If it is part of the picture, you will explore it together. If it is not, it will not become the focus.

Common questions

Does Anne offer couples or relationship therapy?

Anne offers individual therapy. Many clients use individual sessions to work on their relationships, partners, and polycules.

Will I have to explain kink or polyamory terms?

No. Use your own language. If a term is new to Anne, she will ask about it with curiosity.
