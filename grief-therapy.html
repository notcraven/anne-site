Grief and loss

Grief and loss therapy in Grand Blanc, Michigan

Grief keeps its own pace. Therapy with Anne gives you room to miss what you have lost, to feel what you feel, and to find a way to carry it, with no timeline and no stages you are supposed to pass through in order.

Call (810) 246-1948 Email Anne

Losses people bring to therapy

  • The death of a partner, parent, child, friend, or chosen family member
  • The loss of a pet
  • Anticipatory grief while someone you love is ill or in hospice
  • Divorce, breakups, and estrangement
  • Loss of health, ability, or fertility
  • Grief other people do not recognize, such as the loss of an ex, a pregnancy, or a relationship you could not be open about

Experience at the end of life

Anne has worked with clients receiving hospice care and with the people around them. She is comfortable talking about death, dying, and the hard and practical parts of caring for someone at the end of life.

When to reach out

There is no wrong time. People often reach out when grief keeps them from sleeping, working, or connecting with others, when they feel numb or stuck, when guilt or regret will not settle, or when anniversaries and holidays hit harder than expected.

Common questions

Is it normal to still be grieving after a year or more?

Yes. Grief does not end on a schedule. Many people find that it changes shape over time, and therapy can help you live alongside it.

Can I come to therapy before a loss happens?

Yes. Anticipatory grief is real grief, and support while someone is ill or in hospice can help you through what is happening now.
