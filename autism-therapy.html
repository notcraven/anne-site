Neurodivergent-affirming care

Autism-affirming therapy in Grand Blanc, Michigan

Therapy with Anne honors how your brain works. The goal is a life that fits you, with less exhaustion, more ease, and support on your own terms. Nobody is trying to make you look less autistic.

Call (810) 246-1948 Email Anne

Who this is for

Autistic teens and adults, including people who were identified later in life and people who are still wondering whether they are autistic. You do not need a formal diagnosis to start therapy or to be taken seriously.

What people often bring to sessions

  • Burnout and the exhaustion of masking at work, school, or home
  • Sensory overload, meltdowns, and shutdowns
  • Anxiety and depression that come from living in a world built for other brains
  • Misunderstandings in relationships, friendships, and workplaces
  • Making sense of a recent diagnosis or a lifetime of feeling different
  • Executive function, routines, and transitions

How Anne works

Anne starts from your strengths and what you want to change, if anything. Sessions are direct and clear, and you are welcome to say what helps you communicate best. Sessions can adapt, with more structure or less, breaks when you need them, or telehealth from a space that feels comfortable.

There is no pressure to make eye contact, sit still, or explain yourself in a way that does not fit how you think.

Common questions

Does Anne provide autism evaluations?

Anne does not do formal autism evaluations herself, and you do not need one to start therapy. If you would like an evaluation, she can refer you to providers who offer them.

Can I do sessions by telehealth?

Yes. Many autistic clients prefer telehealth from their own space. Anne sees clients by secure video anywhere in Michigan, and in person in Grand Blanc.
